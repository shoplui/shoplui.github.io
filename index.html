Mr CLV youtube upload

Mr. C'est la Vie is a YouTube channel of animated dark comedy: a weekly episode, short clips from it, daily sketches and comedy songs about life's small defeats.

Mr CLV youtube upload is the channel's own publishing tool. It uploads the videos the channel makes to the channel itself, with their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, subtitles and publishing times, using the YouTube Data API.

Who uses it

Only the owner of the Mr. C'est la Vie channel. It is not offered to anyone else and does not act on any other YouTube account.

What it does with YouTube

  • Uploads Mr. C'est la Vie's videos to the Mr. C'est la Vie channel.
  • Sets each video's details: title, description, tags, thumbnail, subtitles, language and scheduled publishing time.
  • Adds them to the channel's own playlists.
  • Reads public statistics of the channel's own videos (views and likes) to see what viewers enjoy.

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. How the tool handles data is set out in its privacy policy.
